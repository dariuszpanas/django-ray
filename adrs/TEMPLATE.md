## Context and scope

Describe the problem, constraints, affected users, and explicit non-goals.

## Decision

State the proposed choice and its boundaries. Acceptance records agreement on
this choice; it does not change the implementation status.

## Alternatives considered

Describe credible alternatives and why this choice is preferred.

## Consequences and rollout

Record benefits, costs, compatibility, migrations, operational requirements,
rollback, and any conditions that must hold before activation.

which parts are replaced if supersession is partial. Keep the original rationale.
